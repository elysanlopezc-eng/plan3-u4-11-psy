Stage I: Discussion Norms & Core Concepts

Safe Space & Definitions

Class Agreements (5 mins)

To build a respectful classroom space, we commit to:

Privacy & Respect: What is shared in class stays in class; no personal stories out of context.
No Maturity Shaming: Scientific, clear language replaces jokes or discomfort.

Core Foundations (10 mins)

Body Autonomy

The fundamental right to control your own body, decisions, and physical boundaries without coercion.

Boundaries & Privacy

Limits set to protect physical, emotional, and digital well-being, honoring personal values.

Stage II: Communicating Personal Boundaries

Individual Skill Lab

Analyze effective communication scripts for establishing and asserting healthy boundaries in various life contexts.

1. Clear Verbal Scripting

Use "I" Statements:

"I feel uncomfortable when... I need you to respect my privacy by..."

Key takeaway: Clear boundaries are kind, direct, and unambiguous.

2. Digital Privacy Shielding

Online & Social Media Rules:

Never share private images, passwords, or personal content without explicit, uncoerced consent. Digital consent can be revoked at any time.

3. Peer Influence Response

The "Broken Record" Technique:

Repeat your firm stance calmly without needing to over-justify: "No thanks, that goes against my personal boundaries."

4. Active Listening & Respect

Montano Habit 5: Seek First to Understand. When someone sets a boundary with you, thank them for their honesty and adjust your behavior immediately.

Stage III: Respect & Boundaries Case Study

Teams of 3 - 4
Case Scenario: Digital Privacy Line

Carlos takes a funny, slightly embarrassing photo of his friend Liam during study group and posts it to a group story without asking. When Liam asks him to delete it, Carlos laughs and says, "Relax, it's just a joke among friends!" Liam feels embarrassed and annoyed that his privacy was ignored.

Team Task (10 mins):

  1. Which boundary principle (Body Autonomy, Digital Privacy, Consent) was violated?
  2. Draft a script for Liam to assertively restate his boundary.
  3. How can Carlos practice Montano Habit 4 (Think Win-Win) and repair trust?

Montano Roles

  • Facilitator: Manages team time (10m) & keeps conversation respectful.
  • Scribe: Records the team's resolution script.
  • Reporter: Presents key takeaways during Plenary.
